Education & Awareness

Recognizing Distress & How to Help

Suicide prevention begins with community awareness. Learning the warning signs and knowing how to listen can save a life.

Warning Signs to Watch For

  • Expressing feelings of hopelessness, worthlessness, or being a burden.
  • Sudden withdrawal from friends, family, and social activities.
  • Giving away prized possessions or talking about final goodbyes.
  • Drastic changes in mood, sleep patterns, or appetite.

How to Listen with Empathy

  • Ask directly: Don't be afraid to ask, "Are you having thoughts of suicide?" Asking does not plant the idea.
  • Listen without judgment: Allow them to speak freely without interrupting or minimizing their pain.
  • Validate emotions: Acknowledge how hard things are right now for them.

Guiding Them to Professional Care

  • Stay with the person or ensure they are not left alone in immediate crisis.
  • Help them dial a crisis helpline like Tele-MANAS (14416) together.
  • Connect them with local counselors, hospitals, or trusted family members.
